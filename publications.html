A Pavement Crack Segmentation Method Based on Deformable Convolution and Enhanced Perceive Network

Paper
Lei Zhao, Longsheng Wei, Zhi Ma, Zhiheng Liu. Multimedia Tools and Applications, 84(19):20995–21016, 2025.

  • Designed the network structure based on deformable convolution and a customized UperNet.

  • Reduced the UperNet layers and simplified the PPM head for local texture details.

  • Adopted deformable convolution for strip-like pavement cracks.

  • Fused features from different heads with trainable channel weights.

Zhi Ma, Lei Zhao, Longsheng Wei. Journal of Advanced Computational Intelligence and Intelligent Informatics, 27(4):561–566, 2023.

  • Designed the network structure based on cross-attention and ResNet-101.

  • Added an edge-sensing module for better edge perception.
